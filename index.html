Interview Questions & Answers

Call Center Interview Questions & Answers: Essential Questions: 1. Tell me about yourself Sample Answer: I'm good in communication as a graduate. I like to work with people and help them solve their issues. I want a stable job and I want to learn English, practice customer service. 2. Why do you want the call center job? Sample Answer: I personally would like to work in a call center to have the ability to improve my communication, enhance my English language acquiring skills and professionally grow where there is defined system. 3. What do you know about our organization? Sample Answer: I understand your company to be a respected name in the customer service field, and that you offer high quality services to businesses overseas. 4. How do you deal with a customer who's angry? Sample Answer: I keep my cool, listen tightly to the customer, say I'm sorry it didn't work and try to resolve the problem with a professional approach. 5. What is customer service? Sample Answer: Customer service is about being of service to the customer, solving their problem and ensuring that they have a positive experience with the company.
